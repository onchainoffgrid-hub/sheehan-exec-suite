What we can give
(without pain)

Short rules for comps & extras. Prefer paid. Free is strategic — not the default.
Pay
1st
He’d rather get paid than free come-here. Comp only for a reason: review, Gold Club, or charity — never because “come to the farm” is easier to pitch.

Prefer this

Extras on something already bought
  • Full Side of Critters when animals are already on the job — low incremental cost
  • Add-ons already on the truck / in the yard
  • Gold card on a locked date (often COMP) — see Gold brochure

Come-here / farm visits

Useful — not the long-term model
  • Great for reviews + proof / volume story
  • Not preferred long-term — push off-site / mobile
  • Free farm visit = strategic tool, not the menu

Do / don’t · 10-second check

Do

Comp an extra already staged · COMP Gold when date is locked · Point charity to Violet · Ask for the review when you give

Don’t

Default to free come-here · Dump Violet pricing in the middle of a consumer close · Comp with no ask (review / next book / Gold)

Separate class
Violet / charity = 50% posture

Point them there — don’t dump the full charity playbook into a Gold or mobile close. Different lane.
