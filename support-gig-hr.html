Not legal advice. Tax / worker-classification rules change — confirm with a CPA or employment counsel before you rely on this page.

What this is / isn’t

Is: gig / 1099-style helpers and partners who help deliver petting-zoo and farm gigs.

Isn’t: a promise of W-2 employment, benefits, or guaranteed hours.

If we pay a worker more than $600 in a calendar year, we issue a 1099. Worker must provide a W-9 (and any other info we need to file) before we can pay cleanly.
